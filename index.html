    T = 1 / f = 500 ms
    The period: time between one kick and the next. Shrink it far enough and rhythm turns into pitch.
    n = 12 · log₂(f / 440) = way below A4
    Semitones from A4 (440 Hz), the tuning standard. Every +12 semitones doubles the frequency: one octave.
    tempo = f × 60 = 120 BPM
    A tempo is just a frequency too slow to hear as a note. 2 hits/sec and 120 BPM are the same statement.
